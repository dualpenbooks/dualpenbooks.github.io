Commissioned textbooks and teaching materials

by Stefano Braccini, editorial coordinator

We write textbooks, workbooks, tests, teacher's guides and course handouts to commission for educational publishers, schools, training providers and driving schools.

What we write

  • Textbooks, complete or in part, from primary to secondary school, following the national curriculum and the table of contents agreed with you.
  • Workbooks and tests, from the easiest exercise to the hardest, with every answer checked one by one.
  • Teacher's guides, with lesson planning, answers and classroom activities.
  • New editions: updating a book you already have when the curriculum or the data change.
  • Simplified versions, for pupils with special educational needs and for those learning English as an additional language.
  • Course handouts and manuals: vocational training, health and safety at work, in-house company courses.
  • Materials for driving schools: the theory test manual, revision sheets, explanations of the test questions.

And what comes after the writing

We can see the book through to the end: editing, translations into Italian, French, German and Spanish with double revision, layout for print and an accessible EPUB edition, which can also be listened to with text-to-speech.

Our track record

Our catalogue has 45 titles and 265 published editions, and among those titles are 40 technical cookery manuals; the main series is published in 14 languages. Explaining a technique one step at a time, with examples, diagrams and the right words for the learner, is the same work a textbook demands. A piece of technical writing, ‘pH made simple’, can be read in our Excerpts.

How we work

  • Contract and confidentiality agreement before we start.
  • Your name or brand on the cover and the author you choose: the book is yours.
  • A single point of contact from quotation to delivery, with deadlines agreed around the school calendar and the date by which schools choose their books for the following year.
  • Quotations based on the amount of work and the continuity of the collaboration.

If you are a teacher

If a publisher has asked you for a book and you want to write it yourself, we can support you in three ways: an assessment of the material you already have, with a written report and an outline, for €100, which we deduct from the quotation if the work continues with us; written mentoring while you write, at €179 a month; consultancy on the project, at €150 an hour. If you know the subject but lack the time, we write the text and you sign it.

Let's talk

The first conversation is free and without obligation. Send us a couple of lines about the project: the subject, the year group or the course, the timescale. We reply within one working day, telling you what can be done, how long it will take and on what terms.
