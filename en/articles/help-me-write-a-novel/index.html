Help me write a novel: what someone working alongside you does

by Stefano Braccini, editorial coordinator · 17 November 2026 · all articles

"Can you help me write a novel?" is a question asked by people in very different situations. Some have an idea and nothing else, some already have a hundred pages and want someone to read them, some have stopped halfway, and some have the story in their head but do not feel up to writing it. In each of these cases, the person working alongside you does a different job. Here is what to expect and what it costs.

If you have an idea, but not yet a plot

An idea is a starting point: "a woman inherits a villa and discovers that her family has a secret". A plot is something else: who the protagonist is, what she wants, what stops her getting it, how it ends. In this case the person working alongside you asks you the questions that force you to choose, and shows you where the story holds up and where it stalls. At the end you have the plot in black and white.

With us, plot development, in writing, starts from €270. If all you need is a direction, there is the consultation: €150 an hour.

If you are writing and want someone beside you

Writing a novel takes time: at five hundred words a day you need about six months for the first draft, plus two or three for the revision. What you miss most in those months is someone who reads it and is not a relative. The person working alongside you reads the chapters as you write them and tells you where the pace drops, where a character changes their mind for no reason, where the reader risks getting lost. You write the novel, with someone who reads it before anyone else.

With us, written guidance while you write starts from €179 a month. If what you need first of all is a method to follow, you will find it on the page How to write a book.

If you have stopped halfway

It happens often: eighty pages written with enthusiasm, then nothing. Usually the reason is one of these three: the story does not know where it is going, there are too many characters, or the protagonist does not want anything in particular. None of the three is solved by writing more: you need someone who reads it all and tells you where to start again from.

With us, you start with an assessment of the material you already have, with a written report and an outline. The assessment costs €100, which is deducted if the work continues with us. Then, if needed, we move on to editing or rewriting, at a price that depends on how much text there already is.

If you have the story, but writing it is not for you

In this case, rather than help, what you need is a ghostwriter: you tell the story, we write it, and the novel comes out under your name. You receive the chapters as they are ready and approve them one by one, with one round of changes included for each chapter. The novel is entirely yours: byline, rights and earnings.

A novel of up to 200 pages costs from €9,000. A historical adventure novel, from 250 to 800 pages, costs from €15,000 to €22,000, because research into the sources is added to the writing.

Three questions to ask whoever works alongside you

  1. Can I read something of yours? Anyone who works on novels must be able to show you pages of their own. You can find ours in the excerpts.
  2. What will you deliver, and when? The answer should be put in writing before you start.
  3. How do I pay? With us you pay in stages as the work progresses, never everything in advance: you always know what you are paying for and what you receive.

The ways we can help are explained on the page Help writing a book, and all our prices are in the rates.
