A personalised book as a gift: what you can have written

by Stefano Braccini, editorial coordinator · 25 November 2026 · all articles

If you look for a personalised book, you mostly find books that are already written, where the hero's name is changed and a dedication is added. They are charming, but the story is the same for everyone. There is another way: a book truly written about the person who receives it, with their memories, their words, their photographs. Here is what you can have written, how it works and how long it takes.

What you can have written

  1. The story of a life. The book tells the story of the person you are celebrating: childhood, work, the people they met, the choices they made. It is the classic gift for an eightieth birthday or a retirement.
  2. The story of a family. Several generations, grandparents and great-grandparents, the place the family comes from, the photographs at home. It suits a golden wedding anniversary or a Christmas when the whole family gets together.
  3. One period or a single story. Not a whole life, but one chapter: the years of emigration, the workshop, a journey. It is a shorter book, and it takes less time.
  4. The family recipe book. Grandmother's recipes, with the stories around them and the photographs, gathered and laid out.

Two ways to give it

The first is to give the journey: the person receives the news and then tells their story, meeting after meeting, to the writer. The hours spent telling their story are already part of the gift, and often the best part.

The second is the surprise: the voices of family and friends, the photographs and the memories are gathered, and the finished book is waiting on the day of the gift. It is also the right choice when the person does not like talking about themselves: the result is a portrait in many voices, and often, reading it, they discover things they would never have told anyone themselves.

The timing: months, not weeks

A real book takes months: the interviews, the writing, the chapters to read and approve, the preparation for printing. The rule is to start in good time. For Christmas, you start in summer; for a specific date, the schedule is worked out backwards from the day of the gift.

If the date is close, ask anyway: a shorter format can sometimes be ready in time. And for this Christmas, if the whole book will not be ready, there is still the first way to give it: the news arrives under the tree, and the story starts being told after Christmas.

How many copies

As many as you need. With print on demand the book can exist in five copies or in five hundred, with no stock to store: one for each child and grandchild, or on sale in online bookshops, if the family wishes. The book and the rights stay with the family.

When we write the book for you

We do the interviews by phone or video call, at an unhurried pace, and we can also start from the recordings, letters and photographs you already have. We write in the voice of the person being celebrated, or in many voices when the book is a surprise, and the family reads and approves every chapter. The price is worked out case by case and depends on the length, on how many voices are gathered and on the material to include: it comes with the quote, after the first conversation, free and without obligation.
