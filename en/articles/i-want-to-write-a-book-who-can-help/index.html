I want to write a book: who can help?

by Stefano Braccini, editorial coordinator · 11 November 2026 · all articles

"I want to write a book" is something we hear often, and it is almost always followed by a question: who should I ask? The answer depends on two things: where you are now, and how much of the book you want to write yourself. Here are the people you can turn to, what each one does and when you need them.

If you want to write it yourself, from start to finish

A writing course teaches you the rules of the craft: structure, characters, dialogue, pace. It is useful if you have the time and the wish to learn, less so if you already have a clear story and want to work on it straight away.

A coach or consultant works with you on your own book, not on generic exercises: they help you build the outline, tell you where the text gets stuck and help you keep going. You write the book, with someone beside you. With us there is the consultation, €150 an hour, or written support while you write, €179 a month.

If you started and then stopped

It happens a lot: fifty pages written in one go, then nothing. This is where you need an editor, someone who reads what you already have, tells you what works and what does not, and helps you start again. If the material needs to be rebuilt, the editor rewrites it with you.

With us the first step is an assessment of what you have written, with a report and an outline: €100, deducted if the work continues.

If you have the story, but writing it is not for you

Then you need a ghostwriter: you tell the story, the ghostwriter writes it, and the book comes out under your name. It is the path for people who have lived something worth a book (a life, a company, a family), or who have a novel in their head, but lack the time or the pen to put it into writing.

A good ghostwriter does not ask you to know how to write. They ask you to tell your story, and they bring the right questions. The book is entirely yours: name, rights and earnings. The stages of the work are explained in How does a ghostwriter work?, the prices in How much does it cost to have a book written.

If the book is finished

At that point the people change: a proofreader for typos, a designer for the cover, a typesetter for the interior. Then you have to choose between a publisher and self-publishing. Note: a serious publisher does not, as a rule, help you write a book from scratch, but assesses a finished text; and if they publish you, they take care of the cover and layout themselves. If you are looking for a publisher, first read Publishers that do not charge authors; if you want to publish on your own, read I have written a book: what now?, where we explain how it is done.

One person or many?

You can put the people together one by one, or turn to a studio that brings them under one roof. That is how we work: from the first conversation to the revision, from the cover to publication on Amazon and in online bookshops, with the studio's team at every stage. Our catalogue, 45 titles that have become 265 editions, is proof of the craft: we have travelled the road from idea to real book many times.

How to choose

Three questions to ask whoever will write or revise your book, before you sign:

  1. What will you deliver, and when? It must be written in the contract.
  2. How do I pay? Be wary of anyone who wants everything up front. With us you pay in instalments, as the work progresses.
  3. Can I see how you write? Ask for excerpts. You can read the excerpts from our books.
