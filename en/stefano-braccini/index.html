Stefano Braccini

Editorial coordinator at DualPen Books

I oversee every project at the studio, from the first conversation to the published book. Whoever entrusts us with a story, a manual or a novel has a single point of contact, and that is me: I coordinate the pens of the house and choose who works on each book.

I work and write across several literary genres. I handle biographies and family memoirs personally: I listen to the person telling the story, gather the memories and the family documents, and turn them into a book that remains yours, in your words and your voice.

Before writing for others, we built our own catalogue at the studio: a historical saga in ten novels, published in three languages, and forty technical cookery manuals, whose main series can now be read in fourteen languages.

In all, 45 titles have become 265 editions, in 3 formats: ebook, print and audiobook. We have prepared 809 publishing listings, and 402 of them are online today, with international distribution. You will find them all in the catalogue, store by store.

I know what it means to start from an idea and arrive at a real book: the blank page, the structure to find, the chapter to rewrite, then the cover, the layout and the day the book appears in the stores. It is a road we have travelled 265 times.

When you tell me your story, we put at its service the same craft that built the catalogue, and together we decide how to turn it into a book.
