What goes into a listing

Every row in the table is a form of its own: the cover in the size that store wants, the description, the categories, the keywords, the price for each market, the ISBN, the rights. Then the file gets tested on a reader, and if a typo turns up the whole round starts again.

This is the work that sits between a finished manuscript and a book someone can buy. It is not writing, and almost nobody explains it to a first-time author.
