How much does it cost to have a song written? The real figures

by Giovanni Casali, editorial coordinator

Lyrics written to order start at 500 euros. An EP of three tracks at 1,200, a whole album at 3,500. Below you will find what the price depends on, and what you need beyond the words to have a finished song.

What the price depends on

How much material you bring. The expensive part is not finding the rhymes: it is understanding who you are. If you arrive with your story already in order, it goes quickly. If the lyrics have to be built from nothing, it takes more work and it shows in the price.

How many tracks. The brief is done once: understanding how you speak, where you come from and what you want to say serves one track as well as ten. That is why three tracks cost less than three times one.

Whether you need it in another language. A singable translation starts at 450 euros a track, and costs more than writing a new one: sense, stress and rhyme all have to work at once.

What you need beyond the words

This is where an honest quote parts company with a nasty surprise. We write the words. To have a song people can listen to you need two more things, and you pay for them elsewhere.

The beat. If you took it from YouTube among the so-called type beats, the free download is the version with the producer’s tag over it, fine for trying things out. To release properly you buy the licence from the producer: it starts at about thirty euros. You buy it, in your own name, so it stays yours.

The voice. Recording, mixing and mastering are done by a studio, or by you if you are set up for it. We deliver the lyrics cut to the metre of your beat, bar by bar, marked with where each section comes in: whoever records has nothing to rework.

Quick questions

Do you make the song, sung?

We deliver the lyrics, ready to sing and cut to your beat. You bring the voice, in a studio or at home.

Can I send you tracks I like as a reference?

Yes, they help us hear the sound you have in mind. Your lyrics we write from scratch: those stay a reference for style.

How long does it take?

For a single track, usually two weeks between the brief and the final lyrics.
